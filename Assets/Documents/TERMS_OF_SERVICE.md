## 5. INVITE CODES AND INVITE LISTS

- **How codes work.** Admins and permitted members may generate invite codes. An **organization code** adds the person who redeems it as a member of that organization. An **invite-list code** adds the person who redeems it to an **invite list**, which controls who receives certain event invitations. - **Use limits.** When an admin generates a code, the admin sets the **maximum number of times it may be redeemed**. Once that limit is reached, the code stops working. Codes may also be invalidated when the organization or invite list is deleted.
