Capz does not ask for permission to **write** to your photo library, because Capz cannot save images to your device. Capz does not request access to your microphone, your contacts, your calendar, your precise (GPS) location, or your health data.

- are used **only** to verify your organization;
- are stored in a **private** location that is not accessible to other users;
- are reviewed only by authorized Capz personnel; and
- are **retained for up to one year, after which they are deleted**.

Providing verification documents is entirely optional — it is required only if you choose to create an organization. We do not use these documents to infer characteristics about you, and we do not collect any other category of Sensitive Personal Data for that purpose. If you otherwise include Sensitive Personal Data in content you submit, we process it only as necessary to operate the feature you used.

## 6. HOW WE SHARE PERSONAL DATA

### 6.1 Service Providers

We share the minimum data necessary with vendors that perform functions on our behalf:

- **Cloud hosting, database, storage, and authentication** — our backend provider stores your account data, content, and images and manages sign-in.
- **Email delivery** — to send your one-time sign-in codes and service emails.
- **Push notification delivery** — the Expo push service together with Apple Push Notification service (iOS) and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android) deliver notifications to your device. The visible text of a notification (for example, a sender's name and a message preview) passes through these services in order to be delivered.

Service Providers are bound by written contracts that limit them to processing data for the purposes we specify, prohibit them from retaining or using the data for their own purposes, and require appropriate security safeguards.

### 6.2 Other Users — What Is Visible on Capz

Capz is a group communication product, so much of what you do is visible to other people by design. **Please read this section before posting.** The following are visible to others:

- **Your public identity.** To people outside your organizations, you are only ever your **username** and profile photo — your real (display) name is not shown to them, and it is not returned by search to them.
- **Your real name within your organizations.** Members of an organization you belong to see your real (display) name, in the member roster, beside your messages in that organization's group chats, in event attendee lists, and in the text of push notifications about your messages. This applies to everyone in those organizations and does not depend on any setting. If you do not want other members to see your real name, set your display name to something you are comfortable with them seeing.
- **The "searchable by name" setting** controls something narrower: whether an admin can **find** you by typing your real name when building an invite list or sending event invitations. With it off, admins can search for you only by username. It does not hide your name from the members of organizations you have joined.
- **Who can read a channel.** In an organization group chat, any member who can read the channel can open the channel name to see the list of other members who can also read it.
- **Messages and images.** Anything you send in an organization group chat or channel is visible to every member of that group chat, including members who join later, and remains visible until it is deleted.
- **Poll votes are not anonymous.** When you vote in a poll, your identity is recorded and displayed. Other members of that group chat can see your avatar next to the option you selected and can open a **Results** view that lists, by name or username, everyone who voted for each option. Vote totals update live. **Do not vote in a poll if you do not want other members to know how you voted.**
- **Edits.** A message you edit is marked "(edited)" to other members; they see the current text, not your earlier version.
- **Admin moderation.** An admin of a group chat can delete any member's message or image and edit any member's poll — not only their own. These actions are taken by your organization's admins, not by Capz.
- **Read receipts.** Senders can see when their message has been read.
- **Typing indicators.** Other members of a group chat can see that you are typing, in real time, while you compose a message.
- **Events, RSVPs, and attendee lists.** Events you create are visible to the people invited to them. Your RSVP and your presence on an attendee list are visible to the event's organizers and, depending on the event, to other invitees.
- **Invite lists.** Being added to an invite list is visible to the organization's admins and to anyone permitted to manage that list.
- **Cross-organization invitations.** An admin may invite an entire other organization to an event. If you are a member of an invited organization, your public identity and RSVP become visible to that event's organizers and invitees, who may belong to a different organization from yours.
- **Organization membership and roles.** Your membership in an organization and your role within it are visible to other members of that organization.
- **Organization branding.** An organization's name and logo, uploaded by its admins, are visible to users across Capz — including in lists shown to people outside the organization, such as when another organization is choosing which organizations to invite to an event.

Verification documents, your email address, your phone number, your blocklist, and your notification and privacy settings are **never** visible to other users.

**Blocking.** You can block another user from their profile. Blocking hides that person's messages from the group chats *you* view. It is private and one-directional: the blocked person is not notified, their own view of the group chat is unchanged, and they can still see and reply to messages you send. Blocking is a personal visibility filter, not a restriction on the other person, and it is not a substitute for reporting conduct that violates our Terms of Service.

## 7. YOUR RIGHTS

The following rights are available to **all** users of Capz, regardless of your state of residence. We extend them universally rather than limiting them to residents of states with comprehensive privacy laws.

**You may request to:**

- **Access** your Personal Data and learn how it is processed
- **Correct** inaccurate Personal Data
- **Delete** your Personal Data
- **Obtain a portable copy** of the data you provided to us, in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format
- **Know** the categories of data collected, the sources, the business purposes, and the categories of third parties we disclose to
- **Opt out** of targeted advertising, sale of Personal Data, and profiling with legal or similarly significant effects (note: we do not engage in any of these activities)
- **Limit** our use of Sensitive Personal Data (note: we do not use it for inference)
- **Revoke** any device permission you previously granted
- **Not be discriminated against** for exercising any of these rights

### 7.1 How to Submit a Request

Email `support@capzhq.com` with the subject line "Privacy Request," or use the in-app privacy controls under Account → Settings.

**Account deletion is also available directly in Capz** under the Account screen → Delete Account.

### 7.2 What Deletion Does and Does Not Remove

When you delete your account, it immediately enters a **pending deletion** state: your profile stops being visible to other users, and where your name previously appeared you are shown as "Deleted User." After a limited recovery window of approximately **30 days**, your account and associated Personal Data are permanently removed from active systems, and from backups within 90 days.

Some information necessarily survives your deletion, and you should understand this before deleting:

- **Messages, images, and polls you sent to a group chat** may remain visible to the other members of that group chat, shown as sent by a deleted user. Content you sent to a shared space cannot be un-received.
- **Poll votes you cast** continue to count toward that poll's totals.
- **Copies other users saved or screenshotted** are outside our control (see Section 6.3).
- **Records we must keep** — such as security and audit logs, legal acceptance records, and information needed to resolve a dispute, prevent fraud, or comply with law — are retained for the periods described in Section 9.1.

If you want a specific message or image removed rather than your whole account, delete it in the app or contact us.

### 7.3 Verification

We will take reasonable steps to verify your identity before acting on a request, typically by confirming control of the email address or phone number associated with your account. For requests involving Sensitive Personal Data or large volumes of data, we may require additional verification. We use information provided for verification only for that purpose.

### 7.4 Authorized Agents

You may designate an authorized agent to submit a request on your behalf. We will require written proof of the agent's authorization and may independently verify your identity and confirm that you authorized the request.

### 7.5 Response Timeframes

We will confirm receipt within 10 business days and respond substantively within 45 calendar days. Where reasonably necessary, we may extend this by an additional 45 days and will notify you of the extension and the reason for it within the initial period.

Requests are free of charge. We may charge a reasonable fee or decline to act on requests that are manifestly unfounded, excessive, or repetitive, and will explain our reasoning if we do.

### 7.6 Appeals

If we decline your request, our response will explain why and how to appeal.

To appeal, reply to our decision or email `support@capzhq.com` with the subject line "Privacy Appeal." We will respond in writing within 45 days, explaining the reasons for our decision.

**If your appeal is denied, you may submit a complaint to your state Attorney General.** Contact information is available at the office of the Attorney General for your state of residence. This right applies in every state with a comprehensive privacy law, including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas, Oregon, Montana, Delaware, Iowa, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Tennessee, Minnesota, Maryland, Indiana, Kentucky, and Rhode Island.

## 10. DATA SECURITY

We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect Personal Data, including:

- TLS encryption for data in transit and encryption at rest for stored data
- Row-level access controls and the principle of least privilege, so each user can only reach the data they are authorized to see
- Private storage for message images and verification documents, served through short-lived, expiring access links rather than permanently public URLs
- Logging and monitoring of access to production systems
- Regular review of vendor security practices
- Incident response procedures

Capz does **not** use end-to-end encryption. Messages and images are encrypted in transit and at rest, but are readable on our servers so that the Service can function.

No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We authenticate accounts using one-time passcodes sent to your email address rather than stored passwords. You are responsible for maintaining control of the email account (and any phone number) associated with your account, for keeping one-time passcodes confidential, for safeguarding invite codes you generate or receive, and for notifying us promptly if you believe your account has been compromised.
